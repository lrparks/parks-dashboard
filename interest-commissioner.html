Overview

The Little Rock Parks and Recreation Commission is an advisory body that reviews, consults, and advises the City on matters concerning the operation and maintenance of the Parks and Recreation Department and the implementation of the master parks plan. Commissioners serve as stewards of Little Rock's park system, helping to shape policies that affect over 200,000 residents.

Basic Requirements

Eligibility

  • Residency: Must be a resident of Little Rock, Arkansas
  • Term Length: Three (3) years
  • Commission Size: No fewer than 5 and no more than 11 members
  • Compensation: Volunteer position (no salary), but may be reimbursed for expenses incurred in performance of Commission business per city policy and Arkansas law

Who Should Apply?

The City seeks commissioners who reflect the diversity of Little Rock. While there are no specific professional requirements, ideal candidates bring:

  • Interest in parks, recreation, and community wellness
  • Ability to attend monthly meetings consistently
  • Willingness to read materials and prepare for discussions
  • Collaborative mindset and respect for diverse perspectives

Conflict of Interest Rules

Per the Bylaws, commissioners must disclose any matter that would have a direct or indirect financial impact on themselves or their relatives. Members with conflicts must recuse from discussion and voting on those matters.

How to Apply

Application Process

Step 1: Submit Online Application

The Parks and Recreation Commission uses the General Board and Commission Application (there is no Parks-specific form).

Apply Online →

Good news: Applications can be submitted at any time, whether or not there are current vacancies. All applications are kept on file for one (1) year.

Step 2: Application Review

When a vacancy occurs:

  1. Applications are initially reviewed by the City Clerk's Office
  2. Applications are forwarded to Parks & Recreation Administration
  3. The Parks Commission reviews and considers candidates
  4. The Mayor nominates candidates
  5. The City Board of Directors confirms appointments

Step 3: Appointment & Confirmation

Per the Commission Bylaws:

  • Vacancies on the Commission are filled by appointments made by the Commission itself
  • Appointments must be affirmed by the Little Rock Board of Directors
  • No formal interview is required
  • Timeline from application to appointment varies depending on vacancy timing

City's Diversity & Inclusion Goal

"It is the goal of the City of Little Rock, Arkansas, that the Mayor, with confirmation by the City Board of Directors, shall make diligent efforts to appoint citizens of Little Rock to City Boards and Commissions so these bodies meet the goal of inclusiveness by reflecting the diversity of our City."

What Commissioners Do

Statutory Responsibilities (per LRC §2-330)

1

Hold Regular Meetings

Monthly meetings open to the public with maintained minutes

2

Advise the Director

Consult with and advise the Parks & Recreation Director; recommend policies and plans for operation, maintenance, and expansion of the park system

3

Recommend Land Acquisitions

In consultation with the Director, recommend park land and facility acquisitions based on Asset Management and Disposition Policy criteria

4

Recommend Fees & Rates

Advise on fees, rates, tolls, and charges for services, facilities, and programs

5

Recommend Rules & Regulations

Help establish rules governing use and protection of the park system

6

Guide Master Plan Implementation

Advise on the master parks plan, including prioritizing projects and recommending capital funding allocations

7

Annual Budget Input

Consult on financial needs and report budget recommendations to the City Manager

8

Annual Report to Board of Directors

Submit an annual report addressing the state of operations, facilities, and programs

9

Regional Cooperation

Cooperate with other political subdivisions on matters affecting parks beyond city limits

Time Commitment

Regular Meetings

  • Frequency: Monthly (minimum)
  • Day/Time: Second Wednesday of each month at 11:45 AM
  • Location: Little Rock City Hall – Board of Directors Room
  • Duration: Approximately 1.25 hours

Attendance Requirements

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Commissioners are expected to attend all meetings. Automatic removal occurs when a member has:

  • Missed more than 25% of all regular meetings in one calendar year, OR
  • Missed more than 3 consecutive regularly scheduled meetings

If removed for attendance, a member may submit written request to continue serving; the Board of Directors may allow continued service by majority vote.

Additional Time Commitments

  • Committee meetings: Monthly (approximately 1 hour)
  • Preparation/reading time: A few hours per month reviewing division reports, meeting minutes, and related materials
  • Special events: Occasional participation as schedule allows

Estimated Total Monthly Commitment

5-8 hours

What to Expect as a New Commissioner

First 90 Days

Orientation

No formal onboarding program; new commissioners learn through participation

Mentor System

Informal; experienced commissioners are available to answer questions

Facility Tours

Not formally scheduled, but can be arranged by contacting the Parks & Recreation Director

Staff Introductions

You'll meet division directors through their monthly reports at Commission meetings

Meeting Participation

  • Voting: All commissioners vote; Chairman votes last
  • Motions: Commissioners may make and second motions
  • Procedure: Meetings follow Robert's Rules of Order
  • Conflict of Interest: Must disclose any matter with direct/indirect financial impact on you or relatives; must recuse from discussion and vote

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need experience in parks or recreation to serve?

A: No. While relevant experience is valuable, the Commission seeks diverse perspectives. An interest in parks, recreation, and community wellness, along with a commitment to attend meetings and engage thoughtfully, are the primary qualifications.

Q: Can I serve if I'm already on another city board or commission?

A: Check with the City Clerk's Office regarding any restrictions on simultaneous service on multiple boards or commissions.

Q: What if I need to miss a meeting?

A: Notify the Secretary in advance. Remember: missing >25% of meetings in a year OR 3+ consecutive meetings results in automatic removal (with appeal option to Board of Directors).

Q: Is there training provided?

A: There is no formal training program. New commissioners learn through participation in meetings and reviewing materials. The Parks & Recreation staff provide reports and context at each meeting.

Q: How are officers (Chair/Vice Chair) selected?

A: The Commission nominates and elects the Chair and Vice Chair at its first meeting of each calendar year. Officers serve one-year terms.

Q: Can the Commission make binding decisions?

A: The Commission is advisory. Significant decisions are recommended to the City Manager, Mayor, and Board of Directors for final action.

Q: What's the relationship with the Parks Conservancy?

A: The Commission has a Parks Conservancy Liaison committee that maintains communication and coordination with the Little Rock Parks & Recreation Foundation (the Conservancy). The Conservancy is a separate nonprofit organization that supports parks through fundraising and advocacy.
